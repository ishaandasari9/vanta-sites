Questions

Things people
ask first.

Short, honest answers. If something is not covered here, text us at (704) 831-0055.

  • What if I do not like the result? +
    You do not pay until you have seen the finished site and approved it. One round of changes is included in the price. If we cannot get it right, you walk away and owe nothing.
  • How many pages does $650 cover? +
    Most builds are 3 to 5 pages. Typical setup is home, about, services, and contact. If you need more, text us first and we will quote it. Sometimes it stays at $650, sometimes it does not.
  • Do you handle hosting and the domain? +
    We do not host your site for you. We deliver the code and walk you through getting it live on something like Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages. All three are free for a site this size. If you want a custom domain like yourbusiness.com, that costs about $12 a year from a registrar like Namecheap.
  • What do you need from me to start? +
    Text us the name of your business, what the site is for, and any examples of sites you like. Send any text or photos you want included. We take it from there.
  • How do revisions work? +
    One round of revisions is included. You send us a list of changes and we make them in the same day. Bigger changes after that are usually a small add-on rather than a full re-quote.
  • Is the $650 final? +
    Most of the time, yes. If the job is bigger than a standard build, we say so up front before you commit to anything. If your budget is tighter, tell us. We work with people.
