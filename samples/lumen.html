Our story

One farm.
One hearth.
Whatever is ready.

Lumen opened in the fall of 2022 as a place that takes care of fifty people a night and not one more. The kitchen is small, the menu shorter, and most of what you eat came up the road from a single farm we trust.

We do not write the menu the night before service. The menu writes itself when the produce arrives. Some nights that means duck and turnips. Some nights it means a tomato that does not need anything done to it.

· Chef Daniel Ortega, and the team at Lumen
