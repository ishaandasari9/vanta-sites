About

A photographer who prefers natural light and the time it takes to find it.

Mira Lang is a photographer and director working primarily in editorial, hospitality, and slow-fashion. Trained at the Maine Media Workshops, formerly assistant to William Park, now working independently from a small studio in Charlotte.

Her work has appeared in Kinfolk, Cereal Magazine, Garden & Gun, and on the websites of small hotels and quiet restaurants across the United States and Europe.
